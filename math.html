Domains in scope

Mathematics shifts character across the 5–8 band: arithmetic and fractions give way to ratio reasoning, expressions and equations, and — by grade eight — formal functions.

OA Operations and Algebraic Thinking
5
Writing and interpreting numerical expressions; analyzing patterns and relationships.
NBT Number and Operations in Base Ten
5
Place-value system, multi-digit operations including decimals to the hundredths.
NF Number and Operations — Fractions
5
Equivalence, addition and subtraction with unlike denominators, multiplication and division of fractions.
MD Measurement and Data
5
Converting measurement units; representing and interpreting data; geometric measurement of volume.
RP Ratios and Proportional Relationships
67
Understanding ratio concepts and using ratio reasoning to solve problems; analyzing proportional relationships.
NS The Number System
678
Extension to negative numbers, rational numbers, and — at grade 8 — irrational numbers.
EE Expressions and Equations
678
Variables, equivalent expressions, one- and two-variable equations and inequalities, systems by grade 8.
F Functions
8
Defining, evaluating, and comparing functions; using functions to model relationships between quantities.
G Geometry
5678
Plane figures, area and volume, congruence and similarity, the Pythagorean theorem at grade 8.
SP Statistics and Probability
678
Summarizing and describing distributions; sampling, inference, and bivariate data through grade 8.
