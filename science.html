Disciplinary core ideas in scope

Unlike ELA and Math, NJSLS Science is grade-banded across middle school (6–8) rather than written per-grade. Performance expectations group under four disciplines and eleven core idea families.

PSPhysical Sciences

MS-PS1 Matter and Its Interactions
Structure, properties, and chemical reactions of matter.
MS-PS2 Motion and Stability: Forces and Interactions
Forces, motion, and types of interactions including gravitational and electromagnetic.
MS-PS3 Energy
Definitions, conservation, and transfer of energy; relationship between energy and forces.
MS-PS4 Waves and Their Applications
Wave properties, electromagnetic radiation, and information technologies and instrumentation.

LSLife Sciences

MS-LS1 From Molecules to Organisms
Structure and function of living things; growth, development, and information processing.
MS-LS2 Ecosystems: Interactions, Energy, and Dynamics
Resource availability, ecosystem dynamics, and the role of biodiversity.
MS-LS3 Heredity: Inheritance and Variation of Traits
Inheritance of traits and the variation of traits within populations.
MS-LS4 Biological Evolution: Unity and Diversity
Evidence of common ancestry, natural selection, adaptation, and biodiversity over time.

ESSEarth and Space Sciences

MS-ESS1 Earth's Place in the Universe
The solar system, scale, and the history of planet Earth.
MS-ESS2 Earth's Systems
Earth's materials and systems, plate tectonics, and the roles of water and weather.
MS-ESS3 Earth and Human Activity
Natural resources, hazards, global climate change, and human impacts on Earth systems.

ETSEngineering, Technology & Applications of Science

MS-ETS1 Engineering Design
Defining problems, developing and optimizing solutions through iterative design.
Note on three dimensions

Each performance expectation integrates three dimensions, not just content.

NJSLS Science follows the NGSS framework: every standard couples a disciplinary core idea (above) with a science and engineering practice and a crosscutting concept. The published reference will surface all three dimensions per standard, not the core idea alone.
