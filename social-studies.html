New Jersey Student Learning
Standards for Social Studies

Grades 3 through 8. Adopted 2020 by the New Jersey Department of Education. Three top-level standards (6.1 U.S. History, 6.2 World History, 6.3 Active Citizenship) organized by disciplinary concept at grade band 3–5 and 6.3, by era at 6.1 / 6.2 in 6–8.

Source: 2020 New Jersey Student Learning Standards — Social Studies, NJ Department of Education. Performance expectations verified against the canonical NJDOE PDFs (2020NJSLS-SS_by_Standard.pdf and 2020NJSLS-SS_by_GradeBand.pdf); 209 PEs across grades 5 and 8, exact match for both code list and statement text.
