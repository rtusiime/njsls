About this reference

This is a curriculum reference for the team designing learning at Forge Prep. It is not an official publication — for the authoritative versions, consult the New Jersey Department of Education.

Each subject page presents the standards in their adopted form, with light filtering by domain and grade so a teacher can focus on what's relevant without rereading the entire framework.

All standards are also available as machine-readable JSON at data/all.json — for use in Claude projects, scripts, or other tooling.
